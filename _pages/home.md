I'm **Boming Zhang**, a computer science Ph.D. candidate at the **University of Massachusetts Amherst**. My work connects AI, educational technology, and teaching.

I build intelligent tutoring tools, design machine learning experiences, and create visual explanations of difficult computational ideas. In my teaching, I combine high expectations with structured support, peer discussion, and opportunities to revise.

I believe ability develops through learning. My goal is to help students strengthen their technical skills and see themselves as people who can continue growing in computer science.

## What I work on

**Learning with AI.** Designing dialogue and feedback that help students examine their own reasoning.

**Making ideas visible.** Creating visual explanations that connect computational intuition with mathematical rigor.

**Teaching for growth.** Building learning experiences around participation, curiosity, and multiple ways into challenging material.
